# What the ente photos show

These notes are from https://ente.com/ screenshots (hero + the Face recognition / Zero knowledge AI pair). They are not a token system. Do not turn them into named variables or a type scale file.

Harshal's instruction: title, short description, visual below; white theme; one accent; rounded containers; lots of white space; playful. The photos are that instruction made visible.

Copy this onto the Phase 1 result card (S1, S2) and then repeat that card. Do not copy Ente's photos, logo, Sign up, or the giant green marketing slab as a case-study background. The slab is Ente's product chrome. Our page stays white with one green used the way the hero uses it: a few words, not a wall of color.

## Saw it

Hero: almost white field. Wordmark left, small nav in the middle, one black pill button right. Headline is two lines, large and heavy. The first line is bright green, the rest near-black. Under it, one grey sentence. Two pills (filled black, outline). Then photos in rounded rectangles, slightly rotated, overlapping, sitting on the white. No card chrome around the headline.

Feature pair (this is the card): two white rounded boards side by side. Each board is title, then a one-line grey caption, then a picture that fills the rest. Title is centered, bold, near-black. Caption is smaller, regular, grey. The picture has the same roundness as the board. No drop shadow. Padding around the title is generous. The two boards are the same shape.

Green shows up as: the hero's first line, small pills on photos (names), and Ente's own product band. We only keep the first kind plus, if needed later, a small green mark like those name pills. We do not paint sections green.

## Worked it out (from the same photos, not a system)

The rhythm is always three beats: big title, thin grey line, round picture. Space between beats is large. The page feels sparse on purpose. Playful means the photo can tilt or overlap; it does not mean extra colors or decoration.

## How to read extra references

Harshal will send Pinterest (and similar) when needed. Color on those pictures does not disqualify them. Dark tiles, a blue field, Duolingo green: useful if the **arrangement** matches. Our live site still ships white, near-black, muted grey type, one Matiks green. Steal structure from the ref; do not steal its palette.

What we are mixing (no single Pinterest tag covers this):

- Sparse consumer landing: big title, one short block of copy, lots of air (Ente hero, Duolingo block)
- Round boards: one fat panel, or a row of equal round tiles, little or no shadow (Ente download / feature pair, App Store cards)
- Picture or illustration as the payload under or beside the type (Ente feature card, Duolingo)
- Pill chrome on the page edge (Ente nav), not a boxed "SaaS dashboard"

Search phrases that get closer than a style name: `rounded feature card landing`, `sparse product page illustration`, `title caption photo rounded`, `pill navbar landing`, `app store feature cards row`, `editorial product section whitespace`.

When a new ref arrives, say which of the three directions it is pulling: enclosed board, open stack, or rail then span.
