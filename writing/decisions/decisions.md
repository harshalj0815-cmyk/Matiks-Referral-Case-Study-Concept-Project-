## Final (site copy)

Some of the important calls sat outside pure layout. I decided referred players should **start with a non-math first game**, so the **first taste is not a cold math pitch**. I decided that when someone shares a refer, it should **go out with the visual** from that moment, so the invite carries what they just felt, not only a bare link. I also decided that if a player leaves the app to refer, they get a **return nudge within about five minutes**, so the loop does not die in another app’s share sheet.
