## Final (site copy)

After the research, the main issue looked like **discoverability**. Few people were already referring through direct conversation, often because they were unaware of the feature, and because it sat deep inside Settings. The interview clusters also pointed at how to implement referral. Users get **excited at a particular moment** in the app. They also want to **share at a particular moment**.
