## Final (site copy)

Quantitative research was a survey meant to **triangulate** what the interviews had shown. Responses came from Reddit, Discord, in-game chat, LinkedIn, and friends. Early replies were few and did not line up with qualitative research, so the sample got expanded. Even then, a lot of people said they **already knew refer existed**, which sat oddly next to interviews where it was **hard to discover**. Some of that **gap may be priming**, or people who don't want to look uninformed. I still moved ahead on the qualitative signal that **discoverability was the real issue** in play.
