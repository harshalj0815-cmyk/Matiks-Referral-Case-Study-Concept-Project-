## Final (site copy)

I spoke with habitual Matiks players I already knew, and others from Discord and LinkedIn. Calls were transcribed, broken into atomic notes, and sorted into **affinity clusters** around share and refer friction. Five clusters came out of that work. The ones I kept were where **friends lose interest** after a bad pitch, and where people stick to **talk or Play Store instead of the referral link**. Refer also looked **hard to notice** in normal play, so discoverability stayed as an in-the-moment issue. I demoted the stronger claim that nobody knows the feature exists.
