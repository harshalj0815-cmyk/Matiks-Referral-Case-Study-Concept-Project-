# Where it sits?

Status: approved · live on case study landing (Feature Context) · visual: /case/where-it-sits.png

## Voice

Casual but professional. Solo: I. Same facts and structure. Skim highlights kept.

## Final (site copy)

For habitual players, the feature **sits in daily flow**, not tucked into a Settings corner they have to go hunting for. The **slide-ups show up in certain situations**: winning a close game, breaking your own puzzle completion record, or hitting a 50 day or 100 day streak. Even in that regular rhythm, the ask is capped so those slide-ups only **appear once a week for any one of the situations**.
