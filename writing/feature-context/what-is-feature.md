## Final (site copy)

Referral here is a **situation-based slide-up**. It asks players to share what they just felt by **referring friends and family with a tracked invite**, instead of only recommending the game in conversation. It shows up in the **regular flow** of play, after a **good moment in the game**, while that feeling is still fresh.
