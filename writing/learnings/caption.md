# Learnings

Status: approved · locked (incl. UISFX + SoundCN) · not in live case study yet

## Final (site copy)

The assumptions I make before starting are rarely the best decision. Streak revoke looked clever until the survey showed people do not miss streaks that often, and the bet had to move to refer in play.

Interviews and surveys can disagree, and that disagreement is useful. Quantitative replies said many people already knew refer existed; qualitative work still showed it was hard to discover in normal play. Sitting with that tension beat picking the flattering number.

Putting the ask at peak moments helps a tracked refer land, but it costs something on the path to the next game. Caps, empty states, and fail toasts are part of the design, not leftovers.

First time I made the prototype with AI and Paper MCP (from Figma), plus light sound (UISFX and SoundCN) and tuned motion, and it made it easier to show the flow instead of only describing it.
