## Final (site copy)

Before locking situation slide-ups, I explored a **wide set of referral ideas** on paper. One early bet was **streak revoke**, where referrals would help restore a long broken streak. There was a browser trial so friends could try before install, and a Play Store trial game option that was removed by Play Store earlier. Another direction was a **Hyrox-like monthly tournament**: an event you enter with a ticket, and that ticket comes from referring someone. More ideas showed up along the way. Most died on research or platform limits. What stayed pointed at refer in play, rather than building a whole new acquisition product around events or trials.
