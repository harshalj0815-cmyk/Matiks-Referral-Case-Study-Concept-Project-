Share the Thrill: Matiks Referral at Excite Moments

Type: Concept case study · proposed feature, not a live Matiks ship
Author: Harshal
Live HTML: matiks-concept-project.vercel.app
AI index: /llms.txt

What is the feature?

Referral here is a situation-based slide-up. It asks players to share what they just felt by referring friends and family with a tracked invite, instead of only recommending the game in conversation. It shows up in the regular flow of play after a good moment.

Triggers: close-game win · personal-best puzzle · 50- or 100-day streak. Cap: about once a week per situation.

Problem statement

A 20 to 25 year old habitual Matiks user struggles with getting friends to try the game (and creating a tracked referral), because they did not know or did not find the refer feature so they only share the app name in conversation or tell them to search on the Play Store, or they introduced it as a math game, so interest dies before giving a try.

  1. Math cool-off (C2): Reframe intro so it does not lead with math.
  2. Name-only handoff (C3): Give a clear get-and-try refer path, not only the name.
  3. In-flow discovery (C4): Findability when they want to share — not “nobody knows refer.”
  4. Reward clarity (C5): Terms / track progress for 150 XP without owning the whole problem.

Final UI inventory

  • Victory / puzzle completion hosts
  • Invite overlays: Pass the Thrill · Brain Leveled Up · 50 Days Strong
  • Pass it on (referred user)
  • Friend arrived toast (150 XP)
  • Track invites (progress / claim)
  • States: Sending Invite… · Invite failed · Weak internet · return nudge · track skeleton · track empty
